Matsumoto

Friday 23rd October
Start: Shinjuku
Breakfast: ?
Stay: Nunoya Ryokan @ Matsumoto ([Nagano, Matsumoto, 中央3-5-7, Japan](https://maps.app.goo.gl/4aZhYQj8BVtysNHr5))
Dinner: Matsumoto
Itinerary
* Board: Limited Express Azusa (Typically departs around 09:30 AM) from Shinjuku Station
* Arrive: Ride for about 2hrs 30 minutes into Matsumoto Station.
* Walk ~15 mins or take a quick taxi to Nunoya Ryokan (中央3-5-7) to drop bags.
* Afternoon: Tour Matsumoto Castle (one of Japan's premier original 12 keeps) and the historic Nawate-dori (Frog Street) and Nakamachi-dori merchant districts.
* Dinner: Shinshu soba and local Sanzoku-yaki (mountain-style crispy garlic-ginger fried chicken).

Saturday 24th October
Start: Matsumoto
Breakfast: Matsumoto
Stay: Nunoya Ryokan @ Matsumoto
Dinner: Matsumoto
Itinerary
* Day Trip Option: Take the Matsumoto Dentetsu Kamikochi Line to Shin-Shimashima, then direct bus into the Kamikochi Valley (approx. 1 hr 40 mins total). Hike the flat, scenic trails along the Azusa River between Taisho Pond, Kappa Bridge, and Myojin Pond under vibrant autumn foliage.
* Evening: Return to Matsumoto; relax at ryokan or try a local craft beer pub (e.g., Matsumoto Brewery Tap Room).
* Dinner: Local Shinshu beef teppanyaki or horsemeat sashimi (basashi).
* Stay: Nunoya Ryokan, Matsumoto.
