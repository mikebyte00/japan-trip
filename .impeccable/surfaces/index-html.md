Scope: the trip map app shell (index.html): map, daily card sheet, day navigation. Mode: Operate.
Job: glance at today's stay, timed items and travel; step through days; plan on the tablet.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The trip as a hand-dyed tenugui unrolled over the map. Refuses the stock map-app look of white cards and blue pins.
OWN-WORLD: Indigo-dyed fields (ai) for the shell (the top chip, the sheet's header band, the pins, the route) over undyed cotton (kinari). Persimmon (kaki) only for timed items and the current pin. Each region has its own small stencil repeat (waves for the coast, hemp leaf for the old road, mountain peaks for Matsumoto and Hakone, shippō rings for Kansai) printed in the band at low contrast. Kaisei Decol for titles; Zen Kaku Gothic New for UI text.
STORY: Open the app, see today's stay and the next timed item, and know where you're going. Swipe through the days and the route unspools.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed muted map with an indigo route and pins. The current pin is persimmon. Bottom sheet: an indigo stencil band holds the date and region; a cotton body holds the title, the stay and the persimmon "next" row. Prev/next and day ticks sit at the bottom.
FORM: tenugui katazome, candidate 5 of 7, seed 61872f23 (degraded roll).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
